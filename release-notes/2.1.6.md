PB clips and pop-ups no longer overstate how much you beat your best by.

### PB gain fixed after a Windows reinstall

If you reinstalled Windows, a new PB could show a gain measured against your
best since the reinstall instead of your real best (for example "PB +294" when
you beat it by 156). The autoclipper and the New PB pop-up now go by your best
on KovaaK's. When the app can't tell what you beat, the clip just says "PB".
