Two new autoclipper switches to keep throwaway takes out of your clips folder.

### Skip first tries

Turn it on in Clips and your first run of each scenario after opening KovaaK's
isn't clipped, so a warm-up PB on a fresh install doesn't fill the folder.

### Keep only the latest PB

Beat your PB three times in one session and you get three clips. With this on,
a new PB clip moves your earlier PB clips of that scenario from the same session
to the Recycle Bin. Rank-up clips always stay. A clip that's open in a player is
left where it is.

Both are off by default.
