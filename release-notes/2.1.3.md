A small release about your best scores.

### Your real best, not just what's on this PC

Until now the app only knew your best from the stats files on this PC. After a
Windows reinstall, or on a new PC, those files are gone, so:

- Snipe alerts said a friend beat your best when they hadn't. A friend's 229
  showed as beating "your 185" even though your real PB was higher.
- Runs that beat only what was left on this PC popped a **New PB** and, with PB
  clipping on, saved a clip.

The app now also checks your best on KovaaK's leaderboard and uses whichever is
higher. This needs your KovaaK's account linked on your kova profile. Without
it, the app keeps going by the files on this PC as before.
