### KovaPresets is now kova companion

Same app, new name, and a lot more in it. Presets, the HUD editor and the stream
overlay are still here and behave exactly as before. They now sit in a sidebar
next to the new tools, and Kovi, the mascot, lives in the title bar, the tray
and the empty states.

This update installs over your current copy. Presets and settings carry over
untouched. Only the install folder and the Start menu entry are renamed, so if
an old **KovaPresets** shortcut is still around afterwards, it's safe to delete.

### Sign in with kova

Sign-in happens in your browser on kova.page, so the app never sees your Discord
login. Once you're signed in:

- **Rank progress:** how far each scenario is from your next rank, the odds of
  getting there, and a score graph for every scenario. Cross a rank line and Kovi
  pops up in the new rank's colour.
- **Tournaments:** your upcoming matches, a reminder when check-in opens, and
  one-click launch of the match scenario. Check-in is always your click; the app
  never checks in for you.
- **Weekly challenge:** your team's scenario of the week, its standings and past
  weeks.
- **Snipe alerts:** when someone you follow beats your best on a scenario you
  play, with Play to go get it back.
- **Live setup on stream:** while KovaaK's runs, the kova overlay shows your
  sens, FOV and crosshair as you change them.

Local runs never write PBs or ranks into kova. They only drive what you see in
the app.

### Without signing in

- **Sessions:** a calendar of every day you've played, and a recap of runs, PBs
  and time played when you close KovaaK's.
- **Autoclipper:** connect OBS once in Settings, and the replay buffer saves a
  clip on every PB, on rank-ups or on every run, trimmed to the run. Clips play
  right in the app. The trimming uses a small ffmpeg bundled with the app (only
  LGPL code, details in its SOURCE.md).
- **PB pop-ups** for every new best, switchable in Settings > Alerts.

### Fixed

- KovaaK's writes stats files for scenarios with non-ASCII names in a different
  text encoding (UTF-16), and those files were never read: no PB, clip or rank-up
  for those scenarios. They are now.
- Three theme fields presets didn't carry (enemy body colour on attacks, team
  glow on head and body) are now carried. Themes like Azabun white and
  snopal-clarity look right after an apply.
- Restart game now closes the game itself rather than its launcher, which
  ignored the request.
- A preset captured while a theme switch was waiting for the game to quit could
  rename the theme to garbage when the game quit, and empty the theme dropdown.
- A restore while the game was running could later be taken as your "original"
  setup.
- Imported presets are checked before they're used: only the settings a preset
  manages, with the right types.
